Before every voyage, the ship must comply with international rules. Most of these rules come from the {{IMO}}. A {{convention}} is an international agreement with rules for ships. Each country makes the conventions part of its own law. The cadet must know how officers check compliance before departure.

Every ship has a flag. The {{flag State}} is the country where the ship is registered. The flag State checks that its ships follow the conventions. Inspectors visit the ship and make a {{survey}}. After a good survey, the ship receives a {{certificate}}. A certificate shows that the ship meets a set of rules. Each certificate has a date of expiry. The officer checks that all certificates are on board and valid.

Three groups of rules are very important. {{SOLAS}} is about the safety of the ship and the people on board. The officer checks safety equipment, fire equipment and the safe stowage of cargo. {{MARPOL}} is about the prevention of pollution. The officer checks how the ship handles oil, garbage and other wastes. The ship writes these operations in a {{record book}}. The record books must be complete and correct.

The third group is the {{load line}} rules. The ship must have a valid load line certificate. The officer also checks the route of the voyage. The route can pass through more than one {{seasonal zone}}. The ship must respect the correct load line in each zone. The officer compares the draft with the correct mark. The ship must not be too deep in any zone of the voyage.

In port, the ship can also receive a visit from {{port State control}}. These inspectors work for the country of the port. They check foreign ships. If they find serious problems, they can stop the ship from sailing. For this reason, the crew checks everything before the inspectors come.

Compliance is not only papers. The equipment must work, and the crew must be trained. The ship must be in good condition. A good officer checks the documents and the real ship. If something is wrong, the officer reports it to the master at once. Then the master can correct the problem before departure.

A compliance check needs order. The officer uses a checklist and works step by step. At the end, the officer gives the master a clear answer.
