Ships sail between many countries, and every country has its own laws. For this reason, the shipping world needs international rules. Four international organizations are very important for the merchant marine. They are the IMO, the ILO, the ITU and the WMO. All four are agencies of the {{United Nations}}. Mexico is a member of these organizations.

The {{IMO}} is the International Maritime Organization. Its headquarters are in London. The IMO prepares international rules for ships. Many of these rules are conventions. A {{convention}} is an agreement between countries. The IMO works on {{safety at sea}}, ship security and the prevention of {{marine pollution}}. Three important IMO conventions are SOLAS, MARPOL and STCW. SOLAS is about the safety of life at sea. MARPOL is about pollution from ships. STCW is about the training and certification of seafarers. Your training at the school follows STCW standards.

The {{ILO}} is the International Labour Organization. It works on the conditions of work for all workers in the world. On ships, the ILO protects the {{seafarer}}. Its main maritime convention is the Maritime Labour Convention, 2006. It covers hours of work and rest, food, accommodation, wages and medical care on board. Rest is important for the watch. A tired officer can make dangerous mistakes.

The {{ITU}} is the International Telecommunication Union. It manages radio communication around the world. It assigns each {{radio frequency}} for different services and prepares the Radio Regulations. Ships use VHF radio, satellite systems and distress equipment. All of them need clear frequencies without interference. The ITU also helps to give identification numbers to ship radio stations.

The {{WMO}} is the World Meteorological Organization. It coordinates the work of national weather services. Weather information goes from country to country through WMO systems. Ships at sea receive a {{weather forecast}} and storm warnings for their area. Some ships also send weather observations from the sea. These observations help the services make better forecasts.

Each organization has a different role. The IMO looks at the ship and the sea. The ILO looks at the people who work on board. The ITU looks at communication, and the WMO looks at the weather. Together, they work for safe, clean and efficient shipping. As a deck cadet, you can see their rules in your daily work on board.
